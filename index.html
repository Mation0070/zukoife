    About

    zuko. is where I think out loud about how a continent gets built.

    My name's Zukoifechukwu Obealor. I live in Enugu, Nigeria, and I'm in medical school. I don't plan to practice. I'll finish the degree, but I've known for a while that I'm an entrepreneur first.

    I pay the bills with words. I'm a content strategist, scriptwriter and conversion copywriter for tech companies, AI startups and founder-led brands. I've written launch scripts, ghostwritten for founders, covered YC companies, and built ad concepts for DTC brands.

    No fluff. No hot takes for clicks. Just writing.

    Africa isn't poor because it can't do better. It's poor because a lot of systems were built to take value out of it. That idea sits under almost everything I write here. From colonial trade to the CFA franc, the pattern keeps showing up.

    Fixing it takes institutions, infrastructure and capital, and it takes decades. So I try to think in decades.

    You'll find essays on African political economy, Austrian economics, money and sovereign wealth, power and logistics, and narrative history.

    I'm building too. Aven Media, a video and creative agency. Sweven, photo and video storage you can search the way you talk. Gadaffi, data intelligence infrastructure for Africa. And someday, a research university in Nigeria that can stand next to the best in the world.

    It's a long list. That's the point.

    Thanks for being here.
